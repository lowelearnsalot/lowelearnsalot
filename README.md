## Why This Matters To Me

I build best when there is a clear social impact.

I care about projects that can reach beyond the classroom and potentially affect millions of people. Last semester, I worked on a small medical vending machine prototype called TouchRX. It was a proposed solution to make the CDAP programme in Trinidad and Tobago — where I grew up — more efficient.

It was not about curing a disease or building the most advanced device in the world. It was about access to medicine.

That project helped me realize the kind of work I am drawn to — healthcare technology that connects engineering with real human need.

This thesis is the next step in that journey.

## Pulse Ox Thesis

My thesis project focuses on multispectral pulse oximetry and skin-tone-related oxygen measurement error.

Current milestone:

- MAX30102 sensor soldered
- Sensor wired to an Arduino-compatible board
- Sensor detected over I2C
- Raw Red, IR, and Green readings confirmed
- Python serial connection established
- Sensor data streamed from Arduino into Python

This is still an early research prototype. It is not a medical device and it is not intended for clinical use.

Detailed technical files are being developed privately while I consult with advisors and JHTV about responsible release timing, intellectual property, and open-source strategy.

I do plan on making this as open source as possible, but I want to consult first and release the work responsibly.
